## 🎯 How to Award Points

### Step 1: Ask Customer
"Do you have an account with us?"

### Step 2: Select Customer
Choose from dropdown at top of POS screen

### Step 3: Complete Sale
Process normally - points awarded automatically!

### Step 4: Tell Customer
"You earned X points today! You now have Y total points."

---

## 💰 Points Earning Rate

**Base Rate: 1 point per KES 100 spent**

### Examples:
- KES 500 purchase = 5 points
- KES 1,000 purchase = 10 points
- KES 5,000 purchase = 50 points
- KES 10,000 purchase = 100 points

## ❓ Common Questions

**Q: Do points expire?**
A: No, points never expire (currently)

**Q: Can customers use points today?**
A: Points redemption coming soon! For now, they accumulate.

**Q: What if customer forgets to mention account?**
A: Points can only be added during sale. Encourage customers to always mention their account.

**Q: Can I see customer's points?**
A: Yes! Select customer from dropdown and their points show immediately.

**Q: What if customer doesn't have account?**
A: Offer to create one! Takes only 30 seconds. Go to Customers → Add Customer.

**Q: Do discounts affect points?**
A: Yes! Points calculated on final total (after discount + VAT).
