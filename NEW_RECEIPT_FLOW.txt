================================================================================
                         TYPICAL WORKFLOW
================================================================================

    FAST CHECKOUT (Most Common):
    ────────────────────────────
    1. Complete sale
    2. Receipt appears
    3. Press 'P' to print
    4. Press 'N' for next customer
    
    Time: ~2 seconds after sale completion


    WITH PDF INVOICE (When Needed):
    ────────────────────────────────
    1. Complete sale
    2. Receipt appears
    3. Click "PDF Invoice"
    4. PDF opens in new tab
    5. Download/print PDF
    6. Close PDF tab
    7. Press 'N' for next customer
    
    Time: ~10 seconds


    END OF SHIFT:
    ─────────────
    1. Complete last sale
    2. Receipt appears
    3. Press 'P' to print
    4. Press 'D' to go to dashboard
    5. View sales reports
    
    Time: ~3 seconds


================================================================================
                         COMPARISON: OLD vs NEW
================================================================================

    OLD WORKFLOW:
    ─────────────
    Complete Sale → Standard Invoice → Click "Thermal Receipt" → Auto-print
    
    Steps: 4
    Clicks: 2
    Time: ~5 seconds


    NEW WORKFLOW:
    ─────────────
    Complete Sale → Thermal Receipt → Press 'P' → Print
    
    Steps: 3
    Clicks: 1 (or 0 with keyboard)
    Time: ~2 seconds
    
    ⚡ 40% FASTER!

================================================================================
                         PRINTING TIPS
================================================================================

    📌 For Thermal Printers:
       • Set paper size to 80mm
       • Disable margins
       • Enable background graphics
       • Use "Print" button or 'P' key

    📌 For Regular Printers:
       • Receipt will print centered
       • Action buttons won't print
       • Clean, professional output

    📌 For PDF Archiving:
       • Use "PDF Invoice" button
       • Formal A4 format
       • Suitable for records
