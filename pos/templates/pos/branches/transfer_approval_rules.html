How Stock Transfer State Flow Works
1. Request / Creation

Branch staff initiate transfer requests. If auto-approval threshold conditions are met, status becomes Approved immediately; otherwise it enters Pending Approval.

2. In-Transit Dispatch

Source branch dispatches stock. Units are debited from source inventory and credited to an in_transit_out balance with deterministic row-level locks.

3. Receipt & Discrepancy

Destination branch receives stock. Full deliveries mark transfer Completed. Short or damaged deliveries trigger Discrepancy Flagged for supervisor write-off or return.
