About Payment Methods

Payment methods allow you to track how customers pay for their purchases. Common examples include:

  • Cash - Physical currency
  • M-Pesa - Mobile money (requires transaction reference)
  • Card - Credit/Debit cards
  • Bank Transfer - Direct bank transfers

Tip: Enable "Requires Reference" for payment methods that need transaction codes (like M-Pesa confirmation codes).
