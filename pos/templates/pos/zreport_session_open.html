What is a POS Session?

A POS session represents a business day or shift. It tracks all sales from opening to closing and generates an immutable Z-Report when closed.

  • Only one session can be open at a time
  • All sales are linked to the current session
  • Sessions must be closed to generate Z-Reports
  • Z-Reports are permanent and cannot be edited
