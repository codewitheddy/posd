How Terminal Binding Works

Each physical register (counter computer or tablet) is bound by a unique device token. Cashiers enter their unique PIN on the terminal to unlock their front office shift session without accessing back office business management tools.

Quick Switch to Front Office

Managers and Admins can launch the POS Front Office at any time using the "POS Terminal" launcher in the top navigation bar. Cashiers can log in directly at /pos/login/.
